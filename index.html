JURISDICTIONS & FORUMS

Frontline Representation Across Subordinate & Appellate Courts

Navigating the appropriate forum with disciplined procedural strategy, guided from chambers in Neem Ka Thana.

APPELLATE BENCH

Rajasthan High Court

First and Second Appeals, civil/criminal revisions, bail petitions, quashing, and constitutional writ petitions before the Jaipur Bench and Principal Seat Jodhpur.

TRIAL COURTS

District & Sessions Courts

Original jurisdiction for civil disputes, property declarations, partition suits, sessions trials, charge arguments, and criminal defence before Judicial Magistrates.

LAND & REVENUE
