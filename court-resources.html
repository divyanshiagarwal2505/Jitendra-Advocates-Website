CONSULTATION PREPARATION

First-Visit Document Checklist

To ensure a thorough and productive case assessment with Advocate Jitendra Agrawal, clients are requested to organize the following four categories of documents prior to their consultation:

01

Notices Received

Any formal legal notice, summons, warrant, police communication, show-cause notice, or tribunal intimation received by you or your family members, along with envelopes showing the postmark date.

02

Previous Orders & Filings

Certified or plain photocopies of previous court orders, interim stays, chargesheets, plaints, written statements, FIR copies, or judgments passed in connection with the dispute.

03

Relevant Records & Deeds

Original or legible copies of title deeds, registered agreements (ikrarnama/bayana), revenue records (jamabandi, khasra, mutation), bank statements, receipts, and identification proof.

04

Timeline of Events

A concise, chronological outline written on plain paper noting key dates, occurrences, payments made, communications exchanged, and names of individuals directly involved.
