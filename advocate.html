LITIGATION & ADVOCACY

Principled Advocacy & Comprehensive Counsel

Advocate Jitendra Agrawal leads the practice at Jitendra Advocates, representing clients in civil disputes, criminal proceedings, and related litigation across the Rajasthan High Court and District Courts.

Based at Bajrang Marg, Chhawani, Neem Ka Thana, the practice provides diligent legal counsel, thorough case evaluation, court representation, and formal legal drafting for individuals, families, and commercial parties.

CIVIL & PROPERTY LITIGATION Representation in original civil suits, property title disputes, ancestral partition (बंटवारा), injunctions, recovery, and contract enforcement.
CRIMINAL DEFENCE & BAIL Trial defence in sessions and magistrate courts, anticipatory and regular bail petitions, FIR challenges, and cross-examination.
APPELLATE & WRIT REMEDIES Appeals, civil/criminal revisions, quashing petitions under Section 528 BNSS / Section 482 CrPC, and constitutional writ petitions before Rajasthan High Court.
CHAMBERS AT NEEM KA THANA Centrally located at Bajrang Marg, Chhawani, Neem Ka Thana, coordinating all regional, district, and High Court filings and proceedings.
