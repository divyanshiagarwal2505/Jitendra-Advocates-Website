Artwork & Credits

Attribution details for the design elements, typography, and original artwork utilized across the Jitendra Advocates website.

1. Practice Emblem & Wordmark

Modern Geometric Emblem: A custom vector emblem designed with crisp geometric quadrant polygons in clean white and champagne gold within an obsidian circular emblem shield, inspired by modern architectural insignia and judicial balance.

Practice Wordmark: Styled in bold, authoritative typography reading “Jitendra Advocates”, paired with the geometric mark in the website header, footer, and favicon.

2. Courthouse Architectural Illustration

Neutral Indian Courthouse Artwork: An original vector artwork (assets/courthouse-illustration.svg) designed to represent Indian High Court and District Court architecture. It portrays a stately neoclassical court building with sandstone columns, grand pediment with scales of justice, arched judicial corridors, and dome in a traditional Rajasthan sandstone color palette.
