APPELLATE & CONSTITUTIONAL REMEDIES

Rajasthan High Court Matters

The High Court of Judicature for Rajasthan possesses constitutional and supervisory jurisdiction over all subordinate courts and statutory tribunals in the state. From chambers at Bajrang Marg, Chhawani, Neem Ka Thana, Advocate Jitendra Agrawal advises on and leads High Court litigation across appellate and writ benches.

Appellate Jurisdiction↗

Civil First & Second Appeals

Challenging civil judgments, decrees, and substantive questions of law arising from district court decisions under Section 96 and Section 100 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

Criminal Appellate↗

Criminal Appeals & Revisions

Filing statutory appeals against conviction, suspension of sentence pending appeal, and criminal revision petitions questioning trial court procedural impropriety or illegality.

Inherent Powers↗

FIR Quashing & Section 528 BNSS / 482 CrPC

Invoking the inherent powers of the High Court to prevent abuse of legal process, quash unjustified or compromised FIRs, and set aside ungrounded charge sheets.

Bail Jurisdiction↗

High Court Regular & Anticipatory Bail

Special bail petitions under Section 482 / 483 BNSS (erstwhile Sections 438 & 439 CrPC) in serious or complex criminal allegations rejected by the sessions courts.

Constitutional Law↗

Writ Petitions (Article 226)

Direct petitions seeking writs of Mandamus, Certiorari, Habeas Corpus, or Prohibition against arbitrary state action, administrative misconduct, or violations of basic rights.

Central Coordination↗

Representation from Neem Ka Thana

All drafting, conference review, document compilation, and filings for Rajasthan High Court matters are coordinated directly from the chambers in Neem Ka Thana.

SUBORDINATE COURTS & TRIBUNALS

District Court Matters & Specialized Forums

District and Sessions Courts, along with specialized tribunals, serve as the frontline forum for trial evidence, original suits, and immediate judicial remedies. We provide vigilant trial representation across all relevant forums:

Civil Courts↗

District Judge & Civil Courts

Original suits for partition, declarations of title, permanent injunctions, recovery of property, specific performance of sale agreements, and execution proceedings.

Criminal Courts↗

Sessions & Judicial Magistrate Courts

Defence representation in cognizable and non-cognizable criminal trials, charge discharge applications, witness cross-examination, and regular/interim bail hearings.

Family Courts↗

Family Courts

Dedicated family courts handling matrimonial disputes, petitions for divorce, judicial separation, child custody, guardianship, visitation, and maintenance claims.

Revenue Courts↗

Revenue Courts & Board of Revenue

Proceedings before the Tehsildar, Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO), Revenue Appellate Authority (RAA), and Board of Revenue for agricultural land disputes and mutation records.

Consumer Commissions↗

Consumer Commissions (DCDRC)

Grievance redressal before District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions regarding deficiency of banking, insurance, healthcare, housing, or goods services.

Accident Claims↗

Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT)

Tribunal adjudication securing statutory financial compensation for victims and families affected by motor vehicular accidents and third-party liabilities.
